## Description (2-3 sentences)
Sasha is a voice-first AI business assistant inside Telegram. It tracks expenses, location, calendar, todos, and reminders — zero setup required. Optional Google Sheets sync. Multi-turn agent, 10 languages, daily digest with anomaly detection, recurring payment reminders. Just talk to it like a human.

## What problem do you solve?
Small business owners and freelancers waste 3–5 hours per week juggling 5+ separate tools — Sheets for expenses, Calendar for meetings, Excel for reports, separate apps for reminders. No single "brain" connects everything. Sasha replaces all of them with one Telegram chat.

## What is your product and how does it work?
User types in plain language: "add coffee $5, create a meeting Friday 10am budget review, make an Excel report." Groq llama-3.3-70b classifies intent, calls tools via a multi-turn loop (up to 10 sequential turns), feeds results back to the LLM, and returns a final response. Supported: Google Sheets (auto-detect URL), Google Calendar, Excel/HTML reports, voice commands (Groq Whisper, 10 languages), screenshot analysis (Gemini), todo list, reminders. Setup: 2 minutes.
